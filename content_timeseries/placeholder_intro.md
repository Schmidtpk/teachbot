# Univariate time series analysis — course overview (placeholder)

This file is a placeholder until the lecture slides (`.qmd`) are added to this folder.
Delete it once the slides are in place.

The course covers univariate time series analysis: stationarity and autocovariance,
white noise, autoregressive (AR), moving-average (MA) and ARMA models, the
autocorrelation and partial autocorrelation functions, model identification and
estimation, diagnostic checking, forecasting, and non-stationary series (trends,
differencing, unit roots, ARIMA).
