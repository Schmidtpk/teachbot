# Lectos — {{course_name}}

An AI assistant for the seminar participants.

## How it works

- **Word budget:** 1000 words of your own input per chat. A new chat starts a new budget.
- **Models:** Claude Sonnet 5.5 (default), GPT-6.1 Terra, GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5 — switch under ⚙.
- **Feedback:** flag wrong or unhelpful answers with the 🚩 button.

Chats are saved and may be reviewed by the lecturer.
